    Settings and local rules

    Thresholds used by the tool

    Defaults: federal micro-purchase $15,000 (effective Oct 1, 2025), the Tennessee central procurement small purchase figure of $25,000, and the $10,000 equipment approval threshold. The most restrictive threshold governs, so enter your district's lower figures if your board policy sets them. Changing a value restarts the check.

    Local rules: board policies, county ordinances, metro code

    Choose a jurisdiction to apply its rules. They are checked at the end of every path, and local dollar limits that are stricter than federal or Tennessee limits are applied automatically. A rule marked Prohibited turns the result to Not allowable. Approval needed turns an allowable result into Needs approval or review. Requirement and Restriction add conditions. Rules apply only to the expenditure types you tick.

    Rules you enter stay in this browser only. Keep the master copy in the workbook.
